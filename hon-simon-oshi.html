About

Hon. Simon Oshi is a Nigerian philanthropist, businessman, and politician from Aninri Local Government Area of Enugu State. Born on 10 September 1992 in Kaduna State, where he spent his early years, he has built a reputation for youth-focused leadership, community development, and humanitarian intervention.

He studied Maritime Logistics and International Transportation at University Cinec Maritime Campus and holds a degree in Business Administration from TanSian University.

Political Journey

Hon. Oshi emerged as the youngest candidate for the chairmanship of Aninri Local Government Area under the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP). He later contested as a House of Representatives aspirant for the Aninri/Awgu/Oji-River Federal Constituency. His political engagements have consistently centred on grassroots mobilisation, youth empowerment, and service to his people.

Business & Entertainment

He is the Chief Executive Officer of Prolific Record Label (Prolifick Entertainment Concept), a platform through which he supports and develops talent in the Nigerian entertainment industry.

Humanitarian & Philanthropic Work

Hon. Oshi is widely recognised for his practical interventions in the lives of the vulnerable:

  • In mid-2025, working alongside Governor Alex Otti of Abia State, he helped secure a state pardon and freedom for Mr. Igbojionu Ogbonna Paul and Mr. Oladeji Kolawole Majasolo, who had spent 26 years in prison. He personally gifted each man ₦1 million and supported their rehabilitation and business startups.
  • To mark his 32nd birthday, he donated a borehole facility valued at ₦20 million to the Nyanya Special Orphanage Home.
  • He has organised multiple food and essential-item distributions across orphanages and has previously provided boreholes, water projects, and sanitation facilities in communities and IDP camps.

His philanthropy reflects a consistent commitment to restoring dignity, supporting education, and creating opportunities for the less privileged.
