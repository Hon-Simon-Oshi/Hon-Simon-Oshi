About Hon. Simon Oshi

A Commitment to Leadership and Service

Hon. Simon Oshi is a Nigerian philanthropist, businessman and political figure from Aninri Local Government Area of Enugu State.

Born on 10 September 1993 in Kaduna State, he has built a profile around youth-focused leadership, community development and humanitarian intervention.

His public engagements reflect an interest in supporting people, encouraging opportunity and contributing to the development of his community.

Community Contributions

Humanitarian & Philanthropic Work

A record of reported initiatives focused on dignity, welfare and community support.

Support for Former Inmates

In 2025, he worked alongside Governor Alex Otti of Abia State in efforts that helped secure freedom for two men after 26 years in prison. He also reportedly provided each man with ₦1 million and support towards rehabilitation and business startups.

Water and Community Development

His reported community initiatives include borehole facilities, water projects and sanitation support intended to improve access to essential services.

Support for Orphanages

He has supported orphanages through donations of food and essential items. A reported birthday initiative included a borehole project valued at ₦20 million for Nyanya Special Orphanage Home.

Support for Vulnerable Communities

His reported humanitarian activities also include assistance to vulnerable people and communities, including interventions involving internally displaced persons.

Professional Background

Business, Education & Public Life

An overview of his professional interests, educational background and political engagements.

Business & Entertainment

Hon. Simon Oshi is the Chief Executive Officer of Prolific Record Label (Prolifick Entertainment Concept).

Through the entertainment platform, he is involved in supporting and developing talent within the Nigerian entertainment industry.

Political Journey

He emerged as the youngest candidate for the chairmanship of Aninri Local Government Area under the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP).

He later contested as a House of Representatives aspirant for the Aninri/Awgu/Oji-River Federal Constituency.

Education

  • Business Administration — TanSian University.
