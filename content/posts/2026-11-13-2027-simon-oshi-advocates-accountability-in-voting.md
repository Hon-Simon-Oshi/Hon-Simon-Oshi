Simon Oshi has advocated greater accountability in Nigeria’s electoral process, urging citizens to assess political candidates based on their performance, track records and contributions to national development rather than party affiliations.

Speaking with journalists ahead of the 2027 general elections, Oshi emphasised that voters should reward public officials who demonstrate commitment to their responsibilities and hold accountable those whose performance falls short of expectations.

He also reiterated his support for President Bola Tinubu’s second-term bid, arguing that some policy initiatives require sufficient time and legislative collaboration to achieve their intended objectives.

On governance at the state level, Oshi highlighted the importance of responsible leadership and effective management of public resources, stressing that citizens should demand transparency and measurable results from elected officials.

He further noted that elections provide Nigerians with an opportunity to evaluate leadership performance and make informed decisions about the future of the country.
