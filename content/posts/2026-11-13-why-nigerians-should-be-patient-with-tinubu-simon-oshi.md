Why Nigerians Should Be Patient With Tinubu – Simon Oshi

Enugu-based politician, Simon Oshi, has urged Nigerians to exercise patience with President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, maintaining that the country’s economic and structural challenges require sustained policies and sufficient time to address.

Oshi, who supports President Tinubu’s re-election ambition in 2027, believes the President should be allowed to complete his constitutionally permitted eight-year tenure, arguing that meaningful national transformation cannot be achieved within a single four-year term.

Speaking with journalists, Oshi acknowledged the economic difficulties confronting Nigerians but maintained that the administration’s reform agenda requires sufficient time to produce tangible results.

Tinubu’s Reforms and the Need for Patience

Oshi maintained that President Tinubu had begun repositioning the country after years of what he described as poor governance. He urged Nigerians to support the administration’s policies rather than expect immediate solutions to every national challenge.

According to him, the process of rebuilding a country requires patience, determination and sustained commitment, particularly when implementing reforms that seek to address longstanding structural challenges.

The Biblical Lesson of Moses

To further explain his position, Oshi invoked the biblical account of Moses leading the Israelites out of Egypt, arguing that difficult transitions require persistence and unwavering determination.

He noted that Moses did not accomplish his mission in a day but had to confront Pharaoh before the Israelites could eventually leave Egypt.

Applying the analogy to Nigeria’s reform process, Oshi argued that President Tinubu needs sufficient time to navigate competing interests, build consensus and secure legislative backing for policies intended to change the country’s direction.

He maintained that national transformation is a gradual process that requires consistent leadership, institutional cooperation and the collective support of citizens.

Continuity and Nigeria’s Political Future

Oshi’s position reflects his belief that continuity in leadership is important for the successful implementation of long-term national policies.

He argued that allowing the administration sufficient time to pursue its reform agenda would provide an opportunity for ongoing policies to mature and deliver their intended benefits.

As Nigeria approaches another electoral cycle, Oshi believes citizens should carefully consider the long-term implications of their political decisions and the importance of consistency in governance.

A Call for Understanding and National Commitment

Ultimately, Oshi urged Nigerians to approach the country’s current challenges with patience and a commitment to national progress.

He maintained that rebuilding the nation’s economy and addressing its structural difficulties would require time, determination and cooperation across different levels of government.

For him, the success of Nigeria’s transformation depends not only on government policies but also on the willingness of citizens and institutions to support sustainable efforts towards national development.
