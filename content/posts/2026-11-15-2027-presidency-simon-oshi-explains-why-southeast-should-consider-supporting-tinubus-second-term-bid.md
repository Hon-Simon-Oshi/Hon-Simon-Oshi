2027 Presidency: Simon Oshi Explains Why Southeast Should Consider Supporting Tinubu’s Second-Term Bid

Former Special Adviser and Commissioner, Hon. Simon Oshi, has called on political leaders and presidential aspirants from the Southeast to reconsider their ambitions for the 2027 presidential election and support President Bola Tinubu’s bid for a second term.

Oshi made his position known during an interaction with journalists on Monday, November 9, 2026, ahead of the 2027 general elections.

He argued that although the Southeast has several capable individuals who could provide leadership at the national level, the region’s immediate presidential ambition should be considered in the context of the current political cycle.

According to him, President Tinubu should be given the opportunity to complete the constitutionally permitted eight-year tenure and consolidate the policies and reforms introduced by his administration.

Why Oshi Believes Nigeria Needs Leadership Continuity

Explaining his position, Oshi maintained that Nigeria’s economic, political and institutional challenges are too complex to be comprehensively addressed within a single four-year presidential tenure.

He pointed to the country’s political structure, which involves different political parties, state governors, senators and other stakeholders with varying interests.

He argued that these competing interests often influence the pace of decision-making and the implementation of national policies.

In his remarks, Oshi stressed that transforming a country as complex as Nigeria requires patience, sustained leadership and a willingness to address challenges gradually.

He also maintained that the President must navigate constitutional and legislative processes when pursuing major policy initiatives.

The Southeast’s Presidential Ambition

Oshi acknowledged that the Southeast has individuals with the experience and capacity to occupy the nation’s highest political office.

However, he maintained that the region should consider a different political strategy for the 2027 electoral cycle.

His position is that supporting Tinubu’s second-term ambition should take precedence over pursuing a Southeast presidential candidate in the immediate election.

The discussion reflects the continuing debate surrounding regional representation, political inclusion and the rotation of presidential power among Nigeria’s geopolitical zones.

The Southeast’s aspiration to produce a Nigerian president has remained an important issue in national political conversations, particularly in discussions about equity and regional participation in governance.

Oshi’s Position on Tinubu’s Reform Agenda

Oshi further argued that the Tinubu administration needs sufficient time to pursue its policy direction and consolidate its reform agenda.

He maintained that major changes in governance cannot be expected to produce immediate results, particularly when they involve complex economic and institutional adjustments.

He therefore called for sustained leadership, arguing that policy consistency is an important consideration in achieving long-term national development objectives.

Looking Beyond the 2027 Elections

While expressing support for Tinubu’s second-term ambition, Oshi also acknowledged the importance of the Southeast’s future political aspirations.

He has previously identified Abia State Governor, Alex Otti, as a potential presidential candidate who could represent the region in the 2031 political cycle.

His position suggests that the region’s presidential ambition should remain part of its long-term political considerations, even as discussions surrounding the 2027 elections continue.

As Nigeria approaches another general election, conversations about leadership continuity, regional representation and national development are expected to remain prominent.

Oshi’s remarks add to these discussions, presenting his perspective on the timing of the Southeast’s presidential aspiration and the importance of continuity in the current political cycle.
