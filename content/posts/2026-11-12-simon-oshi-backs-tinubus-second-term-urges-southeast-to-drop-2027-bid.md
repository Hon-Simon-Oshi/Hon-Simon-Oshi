Former Special Adviser and Commissioner, Simon Oshi, has called on political leaders and presidential aspirants from Nigeria’s Southeast to reconsider their ambitions for the 2027 presidential election and support President Bola Ahmed Tinubu’s second-term bid.

Oshi made the appeal while speaking with journalists on Monday, November 9, 2026, ahead of the forthcoming general elections.

The former House of Representatives aspirant for Aninri/Awgu/Oji-River Federal Constituency in Enugu State acknowledged that the Southeast is home to capable individuals with the credentials and experience to lead Nigeria. However, he maintained that the region’s immediate political priority should be to support the continuation of the current administration rather than pursue the presidency in 2027.

Why Oshi Advocates Leadership Continuity

According to Oshi, President Tinubu should be given another opportunity to consolidate the policies and reforms introduced by his administration.

He argued that Nigeria’s longstanding developmental challenges cannot be adequately addressed within a single four-year presidential tenure, particularly given the country’s complex political structure and the competing interests among political parties, state governments and lawmakers.

Oshi maintained that national transformation requires patience, deliberate planning and sustained policy implementation rather than expectations of immediate results.

He noted that the country’s political and institutional arrangements require cooperation among different levels of government, making it difficult for any president to implement far-reaching changes independently.

Southeast Presidential Ambitions and National Politics

Addressing the Southeast’s longstanding aspiration to produce Nigeria’s president, Oshi acknowledged the region’s political interests and the presence of qualified individuals capable of occupying the nation’s highest political office.

Nevertheless, he argued that pursuing a Southeast presidency in 2027 should not take precedence over what he considers the need for continuity in the current administration.

He maintained that supporting Tinubu’s second-term ambition would provide an opportunity for the President to continue his policy direction and consolidate ongoing initiatives.

A Long-Term Approach to National Development

Oshi further emphasized that Nigeria’s development challenges require a gradual and coordinated approach.

He argued that the country’s political diversity, constitutional institutions and competing governmental interests make national transformation a process that requires time, cooperation and consistency.

His position reflects his broader argument that presidential leadership should be assessed within the context of the country’s institutional realities and the time required to implement sustainable reforms.

Looking Ahead to 2027

As political activities gradually intensify ahead of the 2027 general elections, Oshi’s comments add to the ongoing national conversation surrounding political representation, regional aspirations, leadership continuity and the future direction of Nigeria.

He reiterated his position that the Southeast should reconsider its immediate presidential ambitions and support President Tinubu’s second-term bid, arguing that continuity remains important to the country’s long-term development objectives.
