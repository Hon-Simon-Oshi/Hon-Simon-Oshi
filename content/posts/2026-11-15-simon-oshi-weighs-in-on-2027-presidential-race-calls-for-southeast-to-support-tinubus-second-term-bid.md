Simon Oshi Weighs in on 2027 Presidential Race, Calls for Southeast to Support Tinubu’s Second-Term Bid

As political conversations surrounding Nigeria’s 2027 general elections gather momentum, former Special Adviser and Commissioner, Hon. Simon Oshi, has called on political leaders and presidential aspirants from the Southeast to support President Bola Tinubu’s second-term ambition.

Oshi maintained that the region’s aspiration to produce Nigeria’s next president should be considered within the broader context of the country’s political arrangements and the 2027 electoral cycle.

According to him, while the Southeast has several individuals with the experience and capacity to occupy the nation’s highest political office, he believes the region should reconsider pursuing the presidency in 2027 and instead support Tinubu’s bid for another term.

Speaking with journalists ahead of the 2027 general elections, Oshi argued that the President should be given the opportunity to complete the constitutionally permitted eight-year tenure.

Why Oshi Supports Policy Continuity

Explaining his position, Oshi maintained that Nigeria’s longstanding economic, political and structural challenges cannot be comprehensively addressed within a single four-year presidential term.

He pointed to the country’s complex political environment, where different political parties, state governments, lawmakers and other institutions have competing interests that influence the pace of governance.

According to him, these realities make it difficult for any administration to implement far-reaching reforms without navigating extensive legislative and institutional processes.

Oshi argued that President Tinubu’s administration requires additional time to consolidate its policies and pursue its development agenda.

He also maintained that meaningful national transformation involves gradual processes rather than immediate results.

The Southeast and Its Presidential Aspiration

Addressing the Southeast’s longstanding interest in producing Nigeria’s president, Oshi acknowledged that the region has capable individuals who could provide national leadership.

However, he questioned the timing of the region’s presidential ambition in the 2027 electoral cycle.

He maintained that the Southeast should consider supporting Tinubu’s second-term bid while preparing for a future opportunity to present a presidential candidate.

His position reflects his broader argument that political strategy should take into consideration the timing of electoral opportunities, national interests and the need for continuity in governance.

Looking Beyond 2027: Alex Otti’s Potential Role

Oshi also identified Abia State Governor, Alex Otti, as a potential presidential contender who could represent the Southeast in the 2031 political cycle.

He expressed favorable views about Otti’s leadership and highlighted the importance of visible development, hard work and governance performance in determining the suitability of individuals for higher political responsibilities.

Oshi’s comments suggest that he considers the region’s future presidential aspiration an important conversation, even as he advocates a different political direction for the immediate 2027 election.

A Continuing Conversation About Nigeria’s Political Future

Oshi’s position adds to the ongoing national conversation about political representation, regional inclusion, leadership continuity and the distribution of presidential power in Nigeria.

As political parties and aspirants prepare for the 2027 general elections, debates surrounding the Southeast’s presidential aspiration and the Tinubu administration’s second-term ambition are expected to remain significant issues in the country’s political discourse.

For Oshi, the immediate consideration is the importance of continuity in the current political cycle, while recognizing that the Southeast retains capable individuals who could pursue national leadership opportunities in the future.
